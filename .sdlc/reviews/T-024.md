Assert that "matched multiple entries" is also absent and that 8 GB is used. Add a `vram_gb=abc&gpu_search=0` case as well.
   - Fixed means: each of these queries asserts the used VRAM figure and no search message, and would fail against the pre-change `_resolve_available_vram`.

Everything else in the acceptance list is implemented and covered: the rewrite in `_results_page_context`, the labels, the zero case matching `vram_gb=0`, the exact-GPU and R-003 message cases, precedence of `vram_gb`, `data-box-vram` on the full page, `/results/partials/content` and `/results/partials/summary`, and the chips and links carrying `vram_gb` with no `gpu_search` (checked by page equality with `?vram_gb=12&family=qwen`).
