Why this direction

Expedition makes the verdict the loudest thing on the page without adding a word: the whole right-hand half of each card is painted mint, yellow or pink, with the label, the margin and the gauge inside it, so a scan down the page reads the answer by colour and the shape beside the label confirms it. The form becomes three numbered steps, which it genuinely is (hardware, context, check), in a cobalt hero with a hard-edged shadow language that makes every control look pressable. That is the strongest answer to "generic or dated", and the biggest tap targets of the three for "bad on mobile". Density drops because each card carries one big colour field and one number instead of three small badges. The cost is volume: it is the loudest option, and it needs more pixels above the first card on a phone.

Top Picks: below the first cards (Q-1 option b), as a cobalt band after the last card on the board. In a long list it would follow the first few cards. The first card starts at 668 px at 1280 and 1234 px at 390.
