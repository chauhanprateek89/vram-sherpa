Why this direction

Altimeter reads a model card the way a pilot reads an instrument: one big readout (the margin, in a condensed face), a verdict tag, and a segmented gauge where each notch is a slice of your VRAM and the hatched band is the reserve. The page is calm because the control deck is one row, filters are collapsed behind Filters (0) at every width, and Top Picks move into a side rail on desktop and fold into a single disclosure on a phone, so nothing sits between you and the first card. It answers "generic" with a technical identity (level-meter mark, one condensed family, a proportion bar for the Fits and Won't fit counts), and "mobile" with a card that is already a single column: name, verdict and margin, gauge, then a 44 px quantisation row.

Top Picks: a side rail on desktop (sticky, always open), and a collapsed Top Picks for you disclosure on a phone (Q-1 option c). The first card starts at 642 px at 1280 and 1002 px at 390.
