Why this direction

Basecamp treats the page as a trail guide rather than a dashboard. The answer is typeset first and large in Young Serif, the cards become ruled rows on the page instead of a stack of boxes, and each row gives one verdict, one margin and one gauge, so the page reads top to bottom with nothing competing. That answers "too dense" by removing containers, not content. It answers "generic" with a serif headline, faint contour lines behind the hero and a cairn mark, none of which a default dashboard has. On a phone every row is already a single column with a four-up quantisation switcher at 44 px, filters fold behind one Filters (0) control, and Top Picks sit above the cards as compact rows so the first card still lands inside 1688 px.

Top Picks: above the cards (Q-1 option a). Three columns at 1280 px and three compact rows on a phone. The first card still starts at 752 px at 1280 and 1409 px at 390.
