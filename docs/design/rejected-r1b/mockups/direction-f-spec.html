Direction F: Spatial

Spatial depth. Windows float at different heights above a floor, lift toward you when touched, and tilt a few degrees with the pointer.

Concept. Depth. A back plane and a front plane, with a soft shadow that shows the front one is lifted. The offset is the same lift the cards show on hover.

Favicon. Two squares and a one-step shadow; the blur is dropped under 24 px so it stays crisp.

Files: docs/design/marks/direction-f-mark.svg (header mark, uses currentColor tokens in the build), direction-f-favicon.svg (switches colour with prefers-color-scheme), per ADR-0003. PNG exports (32 px and 180 px) are made from the chosen favicon in the build task.

Glass and blur

Only the header is glass: rgba(255,255,255,.72) light, rgba(28,32,64,.74) dark, backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(160%). Solid fallback #F4F6FC light, #222648 dark, for no backdrop-filter or prefers-reduced-transparency. The header is sticky only from 900 px.

Phone cost. Single column. Windows keep their shadows but lose tilt (no fine pointer). The header is a floating pill that is not sticky under 900 px. Top Picks collapse to one row placed after the first card. Cards keep the verdict-coloured cast shadow.

Blurred layers. 1 blurred layer (the header) at any width.
