Direction E: Glass

Layered glass. Frosted panes float over a soft colour field, and the cards are lighter sheets resting on a pane.

Concept. Overlap. Two glass squares, one for the model and one for the VRAM, and the brighter patch where they overlap is the part that fits.

Favicon. The same two squares without the rim line. At 16 px the overlap is still the brightest area.

Files: docs/design/marks/direction-e-mark.svg (header mark, uses currentColor tokens in the build), direction-e-favicon.svg (switches colour with prefers-color-scheme), per ADR-0003. PNG exports (32 px and 180 px) are made from the chosen favicon in the build task.

Glass and blur

Pane fill: rgba(255,255,255,.74) light, rgba(11,13,44,.82) dark. backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(170%) on desktop, blur(16px) saturate(170%) under 900 px. Rim: a 1 px gradient border (white 95% to 35% to clear in light, 45% to 10% in dark) drawn with a mask. Solid fallbacks: #F7F6FF light, #14163A dark, used when backdrop-filter is unsupported or prefers-reduced-transparency is on.

Phone cost. Blur budget on a phone: 3 blurred layers (header, form pane, list pane) at 16 px. The answer pane and footer use a 86% solid fill with no blur. Header is not sticky under 900 px. Nothing blurred is animated, scrolls with a transform, or sits under a moving layer. Top Picks collapse to one row placed after the first card.

Blurred layers. Desktop: 5 blurred layers (header, form, answer, list, footer). Phone: 3. One static backdrop layer. No blur is animated.
