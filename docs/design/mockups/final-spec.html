Merged, not either original. D's mark was four tiles in a bento; E's was two overlapping glass squares. The hybrid mark is a three-tile bento drawn in E's glass: a violet tile (the model), a tall cyan tile (the VRAM) and a wide outlined bar that is the fit gauge with its filled part in cyan. Violet-to-indigo and cyan-to-teal gradients are E's; the tile arrangement and radii are D's. The header mark has a soft top highlight and a 1 px white rim; the favicon drops both so it stays crisp at 16 px (the filled bar is still readable at 16 px, the tiles stay apart).

Colours are fixed gradients that work on both themes, so the mark needs no theme switch. It is 34 px in the header at both widths.

Why Filters live in the form tile. Filters are form inputs outside the htmx-swapped #results-shell (so ticking a box never loses focus), and R-077 wants tab order to match the screen. D's full-width filter row between Top Picks and the cards would put it before the answer in the DOM but after it on screen. Putting it at the foot of the form tile keeps both orders the same. The disclosure is closed at both widths (open, it would push the first card past 800 px at the reference query); the inputs keep form="search-form" or sit inside the one form as ADR-0006 says.

Top Picks. A <details>. Closed on first paint at 390 px; opened by an inline script after the element from 900 px (ADR-0006 pattern). Without JavaScript a wide screen shows it closed and the visitor can open it (R-078). When R-024 hides Top Picks the answer tile takes both rows.

Focus

A 3 px solid ring at 2 px offset on every control, by :focus-visible (--focus: #4A17B8 light, #CDB9FF dark). Segmented presets and checkboxes show the ring on the whole preset or box; a Top Pick shows it on the whole tile (its link stretches over the tile with ::after). The gap lets the ring read against the pane even when the control's own fill is close to the ring colour. Ring against pane: see the token pairs. Mockup: final-focus-states.html.
